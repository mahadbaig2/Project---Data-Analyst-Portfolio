## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Core Philosophy
The design system positions Mirza Hammad Baig as a senior, credible data professional and BI solutions architect capable of moving across the entire enterprise data lifecycle: `DATA → SYSTEM → INSIGHT → DECISION → IMPACT`. 

The design evokes calm authority, technical competence, and business maturity. It deliberately rejects the common cliches of developer portfolios (dark-mode hacker aesthetics, neon gradients, terminal fonts, floating 3D objects) and crypto/fintech dashboards (volatile high-contrast palettes, hyperactive metrics). Instead, it adapts the refined visual grammar of enterprise analytics environments—such as Microsoft Fabric, Power BI Service, and executive reporting suites—while keeping the human professional, his strategic thinking, and his tangible business outcomes front and center.

### Emotional Target
- **Credible & Institutional:** Conveys that Hammad operates comfortably in enterprise data governance, executive boardrooms, and complex data pipeline environments.
- **Analytical & Precise:** Dense with deliberate information, structured hierarchies, and high legibility.
- **Modern & Calm:** Soft sage tints, deep evergreen anchors, and crisp white surfaces inspire confidence without visual fatigue.
- **Executive-Ready:** Allows recruiters and VP-level decision makers to assess impact within 20 seconds through clear KPI cards, executive summaries, and structured case studies.

### Design Movement: Modern Analytical Minimalism
The system merges corporate modernism with analytical minimalism:
- Crisp 1px structural outlines with subdued tints rather than loud structural borders.
- Restrained, monochromatic sage-to-forest green progression.
- Generous internal padding and strict spatial rhythmic grids, balancing information density with breathing room.
- Subtle physical presence via soft, ultra-diffuse ambient elevation.

## Colors

### Chromatic Architecture & Discipline
The color system reflects enterprise BI rigor: purposeful, restrained, and accessible. It is strictly light mode by default, grounded in a calming sage-white foundation rather than blinding pure white.

- **Primary (`#145C46`):** Deep analytical evergreen. Used for primary interactive triggers (action buttons, primary navigation active state, bold KPI titles, case study links).
- **Secondary (`#68C86E`):** Crisp botanical accent. Applied to positive trend indicators, live badges, visualization sparklines, and active state indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#0F4B3A`):** Ultra-deep forest green. Reserved for button active/pressed states, high-priority header copy, and dense data points requiring maximum contrast.
- **Neutral (`#18211F`):** Deep charcoal with faint green undertones. Used for high-contrast primary typography and icons, preventing the harshness of `#000000`.

### Surface & Functional Tokens
- **Backgrounds:** The primary workspace canvas sits on `surface-canvas` (`#F5F7F6`). Dedicated left-side navigation runs on `surface-sidebar` (`#F8FAF9`). All content containers and dashboard widgets rest on elevated `surface-card` (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Dividers & Outlines:** Subtle structure is maintained through `border-subtle` (`#E1E8E4`), expanding to `border-hover` (`#CAD6CF`) on interactive focus.
- **Soft Highlights:** Badges, active category tabs, and skill indicators employ `highlight-soft` (`#DDF5DF`) or `highlight-tint` (`#ECF8EE`), paired exclusively with `primary_color_hex` text to guarantee WCAG AAA readability.
- **Data Visualizations:** All visual analytics must strictly employ the tonal greens and muted slate neutrals (`#145C46`, `#1B7358`, `#68C86E`, `#82D387`, `#7A8C86`). Avoid rainbow palettes; color must always encode structural meaning, not decoration.

## Typography

### Structural Choice & Hierarchical Integrity
The typography is driven entirely by **Inter** to reflect the operational precision of modern enterprise business intelligence applications. Hierarchy is defined through strict geometric scales, weight contrasts (from 400 to 700), and tight tracking rather than decorative typefaces.

### Typographic Levels & Context of Use
- **Page Titles (`headline-page`):** 36px/44px, semi-bold to bold (700) with `-0.025em` tracking. Used strictly for workspace views and top-level case study headers. Scales down to 26px on mobile viewports.
- **KPI Metrics (`display-kpi`):** 36px/44px, bold (700). High-impact numerical values (e.g., "20+", "4+ Years", "5 Depts").
- **Section Titles (`headline-section`):** 22px/28px, weight 600. Demarcates major dashboard groupings: Work, Systems Architecture, Career Chronology, Thought Leadership.
- **Card Headings (`headline-card`):** 16px/22px, weight 600. Used on individual BI modules, case study teasers, and course blocks.
- **Analytical Overline & Metadata Labels (`label-analytics`):** 11px/14px, uppercase, weight 600 with `+0.05em` letter spacing. Sits above metrics, tables, and section tags to establish immediate enterprise software categorization.
- **Body (`body-regular`, `body-large`):** 14px and 16px, weight 400. Tuned with optimal line heights (1.43x to 1.5x) for dense yet scannable problem statements, architecture documentation, and elevator pitches.

## Layout & Spacing

### Workspace Layout Model
The layout adopts an executive BI desktop paradigm consisting of:
1. **Persistent Left Sidebar:** Fixed width of 260px–280px (~18–20% viewport width) housing the primary profile identity, high-level navigation, social credentials, and rapid skill chips.
2. **Main Analytics Workspace:** Takes ~80–82% of viewport width, maxing out at 1600px with a fluid 12-column grid system.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Adaptive Behaviors
- **Desktop (1280px – 1600px+):** Full two-column setup. Left sidebar remains fixed; main canvas houses 12-column layouts spanning 3-col KPI cards, 6-col analytical cards, and 12-col featured enterprise case studies.
- **Tablet Landscape & Portrait (768px – 1024px):** The sidebar collapses into a sleek vertical icon/identity dock (72px) or a compact collapsible left rail. The canvas adapts to an 8-column layout with 2-column card distribution.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** The analytics canvas shifts to a standard unified mobile stream. The persistent sidebar folds into a compact top identity bar with a slide-over navigation drawer. Grid columns collapse to 1 column, full width, prioritizing vertical hierarchy and touch legibility.

### Spacing Rhythm
Built upon an 8px architectural grid. Component cards feature generous 20px–24px (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) internal padding to keep dense tables and metrics clear and breathable. Page sections are separated cleanly by 32px–48px (`space-xl` to `space-2xl`) to avoid visual claustrophobia.

## Elevation & Depth

### Atmospheric Strategy: Tonal Surfaces with Soft Diffuse Bloom
Rather than relying on intense drop shadows or faux-skeuomorphic bevels, depth is constructed via **low-contrast structural containment and microscopic ambient blur**.

- **Canvas Tier (0dp):** `surface-canvas` (`#F5F7F6`). Receded, calm background anchoring the viewport.
- **Sidebar & Rail (1dp):** `surface-sidebar` (`#F8FAF9`) with a 1px solid border on the right edge (`#E1E8E4`). Zero lateral shadow.
- **Card & Workspace Panels (2dp):** `surface-card` (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px crisp outline (`#E1E8E4`) complemented by an ambient, low-opacity shadow tinted with green-slate:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 18px rgba(20, 50, 40, 0.05);`
- **Interactive Hover Elevation (3dp):** When hovering on clickable project cards, articles, or selectable filter items, the border subtly warms to `#CAD6CF` and the shadow deepens to:
  `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px rgba(20, 50, 40, 0.08);`
  accompanied by a 1px vertical translation (`transform: translateY(-1px)`).
- **Modals & Overlays (4dp):** Used for architecture diagrams and code inspector modals:
  `box-shadow: 0 16px 40px rgba(15, 75, 58, 0.12);`
  with a blurred backdrop filter (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px); background-color: rgba(24, 33, 31, 0.3)`).

## Shapes

### Shape Language & Curvature Calibration
The system enforces a restrained geometric balance that avoids both aggressive brutalist corners (0px) and overly casual bubble interfaces (32px+).

- **Main Panels & Dashboard Cards:** `14px – 16px` border radius (`rounded-lg`). Gives structure to analytics containers while maintaining soft modernism.
- **Sub-Cards, Code Blocks & Inner Widgets:** `10px – 12px` border radius. Applied to inner nested metrics, dataset summaries, and timeline nodes.
- **Action Buttons & Inputs:** `8px – 10px` border radius. Delivers a crisp, clickable enterprise software feel.
- **Badges, Status Dots & Skill Pills:** `999px` (Full Pill). High-contrast capsule shape instantly separates functional tags, DAX/SQL skills, and status flags from square cards and layout grids.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** High-contrast CTA (`#145C46` background, `#FFFFFF` text, 9px radius, 10px 18px padding, `Inter` 14px medium). Hover transitions to `#0F4B3A` with subtle lift. Used exclusively for high-intent actions: "View My Work", "Open Case Study", "Schedule Consultation".
- **Secondary Button:** Surface button (`#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#E1E8E4` border, `#18211F` text). Hover shifts border to `#CAD6CF` and background to `#F8FAF9`. Used for "Download CV", "Inspect Architecture", "View GitHub".
- **Tertiary / Link Button:** Pure typography (`#145C46`, 14px semi-bold) accompanied by a 14px Lucide arrow (`→`) that translates 3px right on hover.

### Card Primitives
- **KPI Metric Card:** Vertical card layout with 20px padding. Houses an analytical upper label (11px uppercase), a 36px bold metric in `#145C46`, a small trend badge (`#ECF8EE` fill, `#145C46` text), and a 13px supporting statement in `#5D6965`.
- **Project / Case Study Card:** Structured container featuring a top 16:9 dashboard preview or technical architecture schema, a category pill, a 16px bold title, a 2-line business challenge summary, technology chips (e.g., Fabric, Power BI, Python), and an explicit impact anchor (e.g., "Automated 40+ reporting hours/week").
- **Insight & Distribution Card:** Compact card incorporating micro-charts (monochrome distribution bars, clean Donut charts in tonal greens) alongside key summary bullets.
- **Timeline / Experience Card:** Vertical left-aligned rail in `#E1E8E4` punctuated by 10px deep-green node dots. Displays corporate role, company, active date badges, and crisp bullet points detailing architectural scope and business decisions.

### Chips & Skill Pills
- **Status / Technology Chips:** Height 26px, padding 4px 10px, 999px radius. Subtle background (`#ECF8EE`), border 1px solid `rgba(20, 92, 70, 0.15)`, text `#145C46`, 12px medium.
- **Filter Tabs:** Horizontal segmented list. Active state uses `#145C46` background with `#FFFFFF` text; inactive state uses transparent background with `#5D6965` text and 1px border.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Dashboard Search Input:** Height 38px, background `#F8FAF9`, 1px border `#E1E8E4`, 8px radius. Prepended by a 16px search icon in `#85938F`. Focus transitions border to `#145C46` with 0 0 0 3px `#DDF5DF` focus ring.

### Data Tables
- **Analytical Details Table:** Clean alternating or cleanly ruled rows with 1px `#E1E8E4` dividers. Header cells use `label-analytics` (11px uppercase `#5D6965`, 12px 16px padding). Body cells use 14px regular text. Numbers are right-aligned using tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).
