Methodology

Professional Delivery Lifecycle

A disciplined, outcome-focused 6-step engineering framework.

STEP 01 help

1. Understand Business Question

Isolating the commercial decisions that require analytical support rather than rushing into vanity reporting requests.

STEP 02 search

2. Inspect Data Reality

Conducting rigorous exploratory data profiling, null diagnostics, boundary condition checks, and schema auditing.

STEP 03 architecture

3. Design Architecture

Defining fact tables, conformed dimensions, surrogate keys, relationship cardinalities, and pipeline orchestration flows.

STEP 04 tune

4. Build Analytical Layer

Authoring performant DAX measures, automated ETL pipelines, data refresh policies, and clear visual UI components.

STEP 05 verified_user

5. Validate with Stakeholders

Cross-validating calculated metrics against operational ledgers, confirming accuracy tolerances, and refining drill-downs.

STEP 06 autorenew

6. Deploy, Enable & Improve

Conducting team enablement sessions, rolling out documentation, monitoring query performance, and continuous iterative tuning.
